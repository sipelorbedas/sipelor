## 📊 Video Production Guidelines

### Equipment Needed

**Recording**:
- Smartphone with screen recording (Android 11+)
- Or use ADB: `adb shell screenrecord /sdcard/video.mp4`
- Or use Scrcpy for PC screen mirroring

**Editing**:
- Adobe Premiere Pro (professional)
- DaVinci Resolve (free, professional)
- CapCut (mobile, easy)
- Filmora (beginner-friendly)

**Voice Over**:
- External microphone (recommended)
- Quiet room
- Audacity for audio editing (free)

### Visual Guidelines

**Screen Recording Settings**:
- Resolution: 1080x1920 (portrait) or 1920x1080 (landscape)
- FPS: 30 or 60
- Bitrate: High quality

**Text Overlays**:
- Font: Poppins or Montserrat (modern, readable)
- Size: Large enough untuk mobile viewing
- Color: White dengan black stroke/shadow
- Animation: Fade in/out, slide, bounce

**Transitions**:
- Keep it simple (fade, cut, slide)
- Not too flashy (avoid dizzying effects)
- Consistent throughout video

**Music**:
- Royalty-free from YouTube Audio Library
- Or Epidemic Sound (paid)
- Volume: Background level (don't overpower voice)

### Thumbnail Guidelines

**YouTube Thumbnails** (1280x720):
- Bold text: "CARA BOOKING LAPANGAN"
- App screenshot or phone mockup
- Bright colors (orange, blue, green)
- Face showing emotion (excited, happy)
- 3-5 words max

**Instagram/TikTok Cover**:
- Vertical format (1080x1920)
- Eye-catching first frame
- Text overlay: "Tutorial 1 Menit!"
